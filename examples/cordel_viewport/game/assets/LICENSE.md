# Fixture provenance

`cordel_scene.gltf` is generated exclusively from the numeric box vertices,
normals, translations and colors in `../../tools/generate_scene.py`. It contains
no downloaded models, textures or other third-party content. Seven boxes,
168 face vertices and 84 triangles; the ground is a shallow box.
